How it works

  • Loadout space starts at 2.
  • Enabling the Soldat class adds 1 space.
  • Rolling the Survivalist perk adds another 1 space.
  • Turn on Fix perk to lock in a perk of your choice instead of randomizing it. A fixed Survivalist still adds its space.
  • Primary weapons cost 2 space; Sidearms cost 1.
  • Open the Weapon pool and Perk pool panels to check or uncheck entries. All toggles a whole list, and the group boxes (Primary, Sidearm) toggle one category.
  • Every weapon and perk starts selected except the Hope Automatic Pistol, which you opt into. Changing a pool re-rolls the loadout so the shown result always matches your selection. Rolling with an empty pool just leaves the slots empty.
  • Modified weapons are listed as their own Modified group in the weapon pool. They start unchecked, so tick them (or the group's All box) to add the variants to the roll.
  • Each roll picks a new perk first, then fills Weapon 1 and Weapon 2 in order. If a slot cannot afford any remaining weapon, it stays empty.
